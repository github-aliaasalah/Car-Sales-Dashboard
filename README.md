# Car-Sales-Dashboard

I'm excited to share a comprehensive dashboard analysis of car sales performance, featuring key metrics that provide valuable insights for stakeholders in the automotive industry


🔹 Highlights:

Total Manufacturers Analyzed: 157

Total Sales: $179.99M

Average Fuel Capacity: 17.95 gallons

Average Engine Size: 3.06L

🔹 Key Insights:

Sales by Manufacturer: Ford, Chevrolet, and Toyota lead in quantity sold, reflecting strong brand demand.

Vehicle Type Preferences: Passenger vehicles make up 57% of the sales, while cars account for 43%.

Engine & Fuel Capacity by Type: Cars have a higher engine size on average (21.90), whereas passenger vehicles focus more on fuel efficiency with a higher average fuel capacity (16.59 gallons).

Top Models by Performance: The Viper, SL-Class, and Corvette models lead in horsepower, showcasing high-performance preferences among consumers.

Annual Sales Growth: Sales have seen a significant increase over the years, with a noticeable spike from 2009 to 2012, highlighting an upward trend in market demand.
